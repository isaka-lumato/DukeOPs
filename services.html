Division 01 · Health, Safety & Environment

HSE Consultancy, Training & Audits

We help industrial operators, contractors, and facility owners meet statutory OSHA Tanzania requirements while building practical, zero-harm safety cultures on active sites.

  • Comprehensive workplace safety audits, gap analyses, and statutory compliance inspections
  • Site-specific Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment (HIRA), and method statement preparation
  • Accredited workforce training: working at heights, fire warden, first aid, and PPE compliance
  • Environmental impact monitoring, incident investigation, and emergency response planning

Sectors served: Construction sites, manufacturing plants, logistics depots, commercial offices, and energy facilities.

Division 02 · MEP & Building Systems

Utility Services (Electrical, Plumbing & Wastewater)

Safe, code-compliant installation and maintenance of essential building utilities—engineered for load reliability, clean water pressure, and energy efficiency.

  • Single-phase and three-phase electrical wiring, distribution boards, earthing, and surge protection
  • Pressurized domestic and commercial water supply networks, storage tanks, and booster pumps
  • Interior downlighting, architectural facade lighting, and high-lumen outdoor security LED systems
  • Sanitary drainage, septic and soakaway engineering, and preventive utility maintenance

Sectors served: Commercial buildings, apartment complexes, warehouses, retail spaces, and private residences.

Division 03 · Procurement & Logistics

Hardware Supplies & Site Materials

Reliable procurement and direct dispatch of structural materials, electrical components, and professional tools—removing counterfeit risks and retail bottlenecks.

  • Hand and power tools: heavy-duty claw hammers, insulated screwdrivers, and cordless/corded drills
  • Structural and finishing materials: 50kg Portland cement, galvanized fasteners, and weather-shield paints
  • Electrical supplies: PVC-insulated copper cables, brushed-metal switches, sockets, and commercial LED fixtures
  • Consolidated Bill of Quantities (BOQ) sourcing and scheduled site delivery across Dar es Salaam

Sectors served: Building contractors, MEP installers, facility maintenance teams, and self-build property owners.
