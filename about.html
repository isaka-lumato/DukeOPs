Engineering discipline in every build and supply.

Founded in Dar es Salaam, DukeOps brings safety consultancy, utility engineering, hardware supply, and civil construction under one accountable roof.

Built to solve fragmented project delivery in Tanzania

Across East Africa, commercial and residential projects frequently stall when safety consultants, electrical and plumbing contractors, builders, and material suppliers work in silos. DukeOps was established in Dar es Salaam to close that gap—giving project owners a single, technically rigorous partner from site risk assessment through final handover.

Our Mandate: Deliver compliant workplace safety programs, durable MEP utility installations, verified construction hardware, and well-supervised building works—on schedule and transparently priced.

Our Standard: Every project we touch—whether a 50-bag cement dispatch, a three-phase switchgear overhaul, or a multi-story commercial build—is governed by documented safety controls and clear technical accountability.

Operating principles

The standards that guide our engineers, safety auditors, site supervisors, and supply desk every day.

  • 01

    Safety Without Compromise

    Zero-harm operations come first. We enforce OSHA Tanzania and international HSE protocols across our own work sites and every client facility we audit.

  • 02

    Verified Material Specifications

    Substandard cables, weak cement, and counterfeit fittings cause long-term failures. We supply and install only tested, contractor-grade materials.

  • 03

    Clear Scope & Honest Timelines

    Every engagement starts with a written scope of works, itemized bill of quantities, and realistic milestone schedule—no hidden variations.

  • 04

    End-to-End Accountability

    Because our HSE, MEP, hardware, and construction teams operate under one roof, we own the outcome from initial site survey to final commissioning.

How we deliver

A structured four-stage workflow designed to keep technical scope, safety, and budget aligned.

01 · Consultation & Survey

Site Assessment

We review drawings, inspect site conditions in Dar es Salaam or upcountry, and verify regulatory and utility requirements.

02 · Specification & Quote

Itemized Proposal

You receive a clear technical scope, material specification sheet, and transparent quotation with delivery milestones.

03 · Supervised Execution

Safe Implementation

Our engineers, technicians, and supply logistics execute the work under active HSE supervision and quality checkpoints.

04 · Testing & Handover

Commissioning

We perform load testing, safety verification, and formal walkthroughs before signing off and handing over documentation.
